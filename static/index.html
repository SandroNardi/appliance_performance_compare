Time

i

Utilization is 0–100, every 20 minutes, for that appliance. It combines CPU use and peak bandwidth use. Only a primary MX returns it.

Performance score is also 0–100, one score per step you choose, for that appliance. A warm spare does not support it.

Uplink is Mbps sent and received on each WAN of the network. Appliances on the same network, including a spare, share these numbers.

Utilization and the score share the top chart. Uplink is the lower chart, on its own scale.
